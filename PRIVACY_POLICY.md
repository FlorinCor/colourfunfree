# ColourFun Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 1, 2026**

ColourFun is a children’s colouring-by-number game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information we collect

ColourFun does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, login, internet connection, or cloud sync.

The app stores colouring progress—such as completed pictures and coloured regions—locally on the device so that a child can continue later. This information is not sent to us.

## Audio and device access

Completion sounds are included in the app and played locally. ColourFun does not record microphone audio, access contacts, track location, or access photos and files unrelated to the app.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ColourFun contains no advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Children’s privacy

ColourFun is designed for young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

## Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until the app’s data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Because we do not receive this information, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
