ColourFun Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

ColourFun is a children’s colouring-by-number game. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information we collect

ColourFun does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account, login, internet connection, or cloud sync.

The app stores colouring progress locally on the device so a child can continue later. This information is not sent to us.

Audio and device access

Completion sounds are included in the app and played locally. ColourFun does not record microphone audio, access contacts, track location, or access unrelated photos and files.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

ColourFun contains no advertising, analytics, behavioural tracking, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

ColourFun is designed for young children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Because we do not receive this information, we cannot access or delete it remotely.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if the app’s data practices change. For privacy questions, please open an issue.
